# Actor Authoring Discipline

What an actor writes down becomes what the next actor believes. This holds for the model, change documents, memory and messages alike.

## Write what is settled

Record decisions that have been taken, states that have been observed, and constraints that hold today. When something is still open, write that it is open and name who decides it — that is a fact too, and a useful one.

## Write the reason, not only the claim

A statement that can be checked will be checked. The model it rests on will not, because it was never written down. That is how a true sentence carries a wrong mechanism past every reviewer: the part that was verifiable was verified, and the part that was load-bearing was invisible.

So write the reason next to the claim — not as justification, but as the thing you are asking the reader to disagree with. **A checkable statement is not a reviewed statement. What gets reviewed is the reason, and an unwritten reason offers nothing to argue with.**

The failure is not that readers stop at the claim. A reader who needs a reason and finds none will build one, and the one they build will be plausible, will fit the evidence, and may be wrong. It then travels with the claim's authority, because the claim was checked.

The same holds for a claim about an artefact — "it is boarded", "it is committed". If the artefact does not exist yet, the claim cannot be checked at the moment it is most likely to be believed. Make the thing, then say it exists.

If you are sure of a conclusion and cannot state why in one sentence, that is the signal: write it as open, or find the reason first.

## Where everything else goes

Capability that is intended but not yet specified belongs in the specification, written at the moment it is specified. That is the exit, and it is cheap: nothing is lost, it is written once, in the place that governs it.

## Sections are the contract, content is the claim

An empty template section says "not yet delivered", and that is information worth keeping. Content invented to fill a section says "this was decided", which is not true — and someone has to find it again later and take it back out.

## Describe a boundary by what belongs inside it

State what a thing is, precisely enough that a reader can tell what it is not. A name, once written, is remembered and acted upon — including a name written in order to rule it out.

## When you are unsure

Ask the actor that triggered the work, or the user. An unanswered question costs one message. A plausible invention costs a correction campaign, and only after someone notices.
