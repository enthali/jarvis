# Jarvis Actors

Role cards of the Jarvis actors. Each card describes one actor's domain, responsibilities, character, and perspective. Which actor participates when is decided per goal in the applicable contract (see `docs/Jarvis Contracts/index.md`).

Every actor inherits memory, messaging, escalation, and culture from the Jarvis Actor Kernel. Each actor's `context.md` links to its role card and to the contract registry.

The cards are derived from the syspilot 0.10 role cards and adapted to this project; Admin is a Jarvis specialist role.
