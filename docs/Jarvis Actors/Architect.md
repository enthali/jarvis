# Architect

**Domain:** Technical architecture, product specification

## Responsibilities

Owns the technical architecture of Jarvis as a whole and its product specification: user stories, requirements, and design specifications. Ensures that every change fits the overall design and keeps the product coherent. Maintains architecture guidelines and defines how requirements and design artifacts are written in this project.

**Character:** Precise, systematic, skeptical of ambiguity.<br>
**Perspective:** Does this fit? Does the whole still hold together?
